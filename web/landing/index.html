PixelEscape

Prototype v0.9 · play with friends · built in Godot 4

Play in browser Download for Windows

Controls

LobbyBuild your character on the left, type a nickname, pick the mode and press PLAY
Play with friendsOne player presses "Create party" and reads out the 5-letter code. The others type it under Join. The host picks the mode and presses PLAY; everyone lands on the same island. Friends can join a game already running. Zombie Royale: fight the zombies together (no friendly fire). Last Standing: shoot each other. Dead players are back after 10 seconds
MenuEsc: pause (Zombie Royale alone), settings, controls, fullscreen, back to lobby
MoveW A S D, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch
ShootLeft mouse, right mouse aims, R reloads
Weapons1 Pistol, 2 UZI, 3 Shotgun, 4 Rifle, 5 Knife, 6 Bat (or mouse wheel)
UseE: open doors, talk to Bjørn, enter vehicles
HarvestHit trees, rocks and bushes with the knife or bat
CraftQ. A sandbag is 1 fibre (bushes) + 2 stone (rocks)
BuildHold B, flick the mouse towards a wall type, let go. R rotates, left click places, right click exits
DriveTalk to Bjørn in the old car dealership (blue on the map) and pick the Tike, motorbike, ATV, sport sedan or the helicopter (delivered to the hangar). E next to it to get in. W/S drive, A/D steer, Space brake, G horn, V camera, E get out. Find the dirt ramps for air time
Developer modeP: zombies chase me on/off, god mode, free building, helicopter (parks in the hangar)
HangarGreen on the map. Get the helicopter from Bjørn. Press E at the red button inside by the door: the roof slides open and the helicopter can fly straight up and out. Space climb, Shift/C descend, W/S fly, A/D turn
BodycamSettings > Gameplay. Night vision: Z
Map and commsM map, T team ping, Y open broadcast (draws zombies)
Test keysK +10 zombies, L +50 zombies, O remove zombies, I stats, U antidote, N +20 sandbags

Modes in this prototype

Last Standing: the Hex Horizon storm shrinks the island, kills plants and hurts you outside the zone. No zombies.

Zombie Royale: zombies idle around the island. Noise and sight wake them, screams pull in more, and hordes arrive over time. No storm.

Windows: unzip and run PixelEscape.exe. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher; choose "More info" then "Run anyway".
