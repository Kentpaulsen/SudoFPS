SudoFPS

Prototype v0.5 · offline sandbox · built in Godot 4

Play in browser Download for Windows

Controls

MenuEsc: pause (Zombie Royale), settings, controls, fullscreen
MoveW A S D, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch
ShootLeft mouse, right mouse aims, R reloads
Weapons1 Pistol, 2 UZI, 3 Shotgun, 4 Rifle, 5 Knife, 6 Bat (or mouse wheel)
HarvestHit trees, rocks and bushes with the knife or bat
CraftQ. A sandbag is 1 fibre (bushes) + 2 stone (rocks)
BuildHold B, flick the mouse towards a wall type, let go. R rotates, left click places, right click exits
DriveThe Tike and Bike are in the old car dealership (blue on the map). E next to one. W/S drive, A/D steer, Space brake, G horn, V camera, E get out. Find the dirt ramps for air time
Developer modeP: zombies chase me on/off, god mode, free building
Map and commsM map, T team ping, Y open broadcast (draws zombies)
Test keysK +10 zombies, L +50 zombies, O remove zombies, I stats, U antidote, N +20 sandbags

Modes in this prototype

Last Standing: the Hex Horizon storm shrinks the island, kills plants and hurts you outside the zone. No zombies.

Zombie Royale: zombies idle around the island. Noise and sight wake them, screams pull in more, and hordes arrive over time. No storm.

Windows: unzip and run SudoFPS.exe. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher; choose "More info" then "Run anyway".
